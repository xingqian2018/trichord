## Details

### Artificial Analysis Leaderboard

- Closely collaborate with @Snah and @Fran. As a team, we fight for the best T2I model submission to Artificial Analysis public leaderboard.
- As a result, Cosmos3 T2I achieves the top position on Artificial Analysis text-to-image benchmark.

### Cosmos3 Paper: Text2Image SFT, Benchmark, Results

- Drafting the write-up for the T2I SFT training section: data recipe, training setup.
- Writing benchmark section with quantitative results: mainly for UGB / CVTG500L / CVTG102ch, and document the philosophy of each evaluation methodology.
- Also help prepare qualitative visual results and model comparison figures.

### Cosmos3 T2I PostTraining [[Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17xcVU5Xj4n5p1mwGv3zETmC5-Xib5RAcFJ9a_LhQSDM/edit?tab=t.0)]

- Worked with @Snah and @Fran as a team, post-trained the model from our GA checkpoint.
- Systematically varied data mixture ratios to identify the best SFT recipe.
- Closely tracked quantitative scores (UGB / HPSv3 / CVTG) on various checkpoints / recipes / merged models, alongside eyeball checks on AA-leaderboard cases. The final checkpoint decision was made this way.
- Some train details, the final submission checkpoint were reached with a 20k+ step sft from our GA checkpoint plus additional thousands of steps for Ultra Quality SFT, early stopping according to highest UGB.

### 3M New Eng + Chinese Text Dataset, and AA-focused SFT Dataset.

- To further boost text score, we built a new 3M-sample dataset covering English and Chinese scene text.
- To further boost preference score, we built a 400k+ UHQ preference data towards top models.

### Post Cosmos3 caption ablation study [[Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/12FydDwgtQKxezGSYv0kdPaMp138SeiXnabmWe4wQfnU/edit?tab=t.0)]

- Proposed a new set of ablation studies to scrutinize which aspects of the caption have the biggest impact on our Cosmos3 release.
- Verifying and investigating prompt and image quality gaps between applying Cosmos3 Reasoner Upsampler vs. Top LLM Upsampler.
- Looking deeper to validate some of the Cosmos3 SFT training techniques, such as caption format augmentation.
