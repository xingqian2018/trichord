## Details

### Image Data

- Scanned the full pretrain/midtrain image database (16 pretrain datasets, 40,658 LanceDB fragments) and back-filled missing taxonomy information needed for midtrain filtering.
  - Bucketed every image by source / taxonomy / resolution / aspect ratio / filter / aesthetic, and built a statistics-based analysis to judge how useful a new dataset is before training. This helps with data-mixing decisions and with catching data issues early.
- Tracked Getty and SSTK acquisition and worked closely with the data acquisition team, sharing experiment reports to support team decisions.
  - Expected volume is ~180M stock photos from Getty and ~530M from SSTK; the final amount will depend on pricing.
  - Statistically analyzed the Getty sample data (resolution, aspect ratio, taxonomy, aesthetic and quality filters) against our existing database to estimate its value before purchase.
- Kept monitoring midtrain data quality, including visualization tools for browsing and checking the data.
- Tracked and organized legal information for the image database and for model-generated SDG data.
- Continued OCR data generation; brought up the infra for two new OCR SDG generators, poster and poster-chart, raising the difficulty of OCR image data another step.
- Helped the team generate Cosmos3 SDG data.
- Studying how to use Cosmos3.5 super-resolution to produce higher-quality training images.

### Image Evaluation

- Kept tracking new closed- and open-source T2I models and maintained the internal benchmark analysis reports (UGB / CVTG baselines).
- Brought in recent academic work on T2I evaluation.

### Image Model Training

- Cross-validated the reported Cosmos3.5 UGB plateau, then joined the discussion on possible experiment issues and how to improve generation quality.

### Language Model (Dense Pretraining)

- Owned the teacher cache end to end: storage, data format, pipeline, data de-risk experiments, and the choice of teacher model version (MiMo V2.6-Pro-RL).
  - Final data plan covers 14.17T source tokens across 1,975 prefixes, with careful handling of special tokens, SFT/reasoning formats, and windowed teacher scoring.
  - Designed and implemented offline sequence packing: every training sample's blocks, positions, and padding are precomputed into a plan file, so the trainer reads teacher rows directly with no runtime sampling or packing.
- Aligned evaluation rules with the team and collected and organized evaluation data.
  - Best pretrain loss is −0.09 vs. vanilla CE, a large gain.
- Tracked and supplemented the team's architecture-mining experiments.
- Work are mostly finished and transfering focus back to image.
