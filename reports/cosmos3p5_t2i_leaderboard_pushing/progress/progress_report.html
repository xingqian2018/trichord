本周结果

  1. 新的 QImageBench（Xingqian Xu 开发）已正式采用，并合并进 data 和 research 两个环节。

  3. Ruofan 用 QImageBench 打分器对 SDG 数据和真实数据做了初步对比研究，没有发现 SDG 数据有显著问题。至少在这个结果上，基于 Cosmos 的 SDG 数据可以安全地用于训练。

  5. Ultra Training 的 infrastructure 已跑通，为更大规模的实验做好准备。

Update (2026-11-12)

Results this week

• The new QImageBench (by @Xingqian Xu) is now formally adopted and merged into both the data and research workflows. [Doc]
• Key finding: @Ruofan Liang and @Jiaojiao Fan confirmed on the new QImageBench that K19 prompt upsampling is still effective. They are now using the benchmark continue finer-grained and score pushing on both open-loop and closed-loop improvements.
• @Ruofan Liang ran a preliminary study comparing Cosmos3-as-proxy SDG data with real data using the QImageBench scorer and found no significant issues with the SDG data. Based on this result, Cosmos-based SDG data is safe to use for training. But further ablations and filtering studies are still requires.
• @Jiaojiao Fan is finetuning Cosmos Reason to check whether the prompt-upsampling data traces are useful.
• The Ultra Training infrastructure of @Jiaojiao Fan and @Ruofan Liang is up and running, ready for larger experiments.

• Continue improving prompt upsampling with open-loop and closed-loop methods: collect and conclude reason ablations (@Jiaojiao Fan)
