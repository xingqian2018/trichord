Findings

  • KD beats CE at every rung, on every validation set, for every α (all 108 cells negative).
  • The gain grows with scale: KD lowers overall_tw loss by 0.041 → 0.045 → 0.049 → 0.052 (q0 → q3).
  • Equivalent size: KD models match CE models with 1.19 → 1.25 → 1.31 → 1.43× the active parameters (interpolated on CE's loss vs log N curve), and the multiplier grows with scale.
  • The gain is largest on uncheatable / codefresh and smallest on val_v2 and private_internal; private_internal is a private set outside the training corpus, and KD improves it consistently too.
  • α 0.7 / 0.8 / 0.9 are indistinguishable (largest gap 0.004); the choice of α does not change the conclusion.
  • Optimizers (all CE): MuonH's overall_tw gain over AdamW shrinks with scale (0.079 → 0.039 → 0.026 → 0.019 → 0.013 (q0 → q4)), while KD's gain grows - the KD benefit holds up at scale better than the optimizer change.
  • KD loss variants (same α 0.8, MuonH): the sampled-tail variants beat B64 consistently, but the lead shrinks with scale (overall_tw, q0 → q2: S64Y -0.0059 → -0.0045 → -0.0029; S64 -0.0023 → -0.0052 → -0.0035; S128 -0.0023 → -0.0040 → -0.0031); the bucket variants are ≈ 0 (B64Y -0.0002 → -0.0017 → +0.0007; B128 -0.0008 → -0.0018 → +0.0001).
  • MuonH learning rate (tsqw, parabola-fitted best multiple m*, * = at the edge of the sweep): q0@9.6B (TPP 200)1.21× (η* 0.0162); q1@9.6B (TPP 62)1.48× (η* 0.0162); q1@30.8B (TPP 200)1.52× (η* 0.0114); q2@9.6B (TPP 27)1.40×* (η* 0.0133); q2@71.1B (TPP 200)1.20×* (η* 0.0060); q3@9.6B (TPP 14)1.74× (η* 0.0147).
