7. Known issues / operations

  • None of Boxiang's runs use the evaluation settings we merged (checked 10-22 18:30 on the argv of all 148 runs): none sets VAL_PER_SPLIT=1 (per-split validation on the 9 decon suites incl. private_internal, with dpl's tw / macro / overall), and none uses EVAL_AT_ITERS / EVAL_TPP_STEP. He uses his own protocol: 6 pooled validation sets (val_v2 not split into cc / python / arxiv, no private_internal), in-loop eval every 3500 steps, doc-isolated eval run afterwards. So the numbers here are not directly comparable with dpl's metrics.
